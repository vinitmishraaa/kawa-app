This unit economics model demonstrates that **formal aggregation through the KAWA platform increases grassroots collector net income by 31.4%**, while recovering **98% of critical raw materials (Lithium, Cobalt, Neodymium, Gallium, Gold)** and completely eliminating toxic emissions.

## 3. Why Authorized Recyclers Can Pay a Higher Price

Grassroots collectors often ask: *"Why would an authorized factory pay me ₹520/kg for circuit boards when the local mandi only offers ₹380/kg?"*

The economic justification rests on two pillars:

### Pillar 1: Superior Metallurgical Yields
- **Backyard Cyanide/Acid Leaching**: Can only dissolve superficial gold plating. It is chemically incapable of extracting micro-alloyed Palladium, Platinum, Gallium, and Tantalum, which wash away in wastewater.
- **Formal Closed-Loop Hydrometallurgy**: CPCB facilities extract **all 6 constituent precious and critical metals** at >97% industrial purity. The recovered basket of metals generates **₹650–₹720/kg of raw material value**, allowing the recycler to profitably pay **₹520/kg** directly to the collector.

### Pillar 2: Central CPCB EPR Certificate Monetization
- Under the E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022, electronic hardware manufacturers (OEMs) must purchase **EPR Certificates** to meet statutory recycling quotas.
- For every 1 metric ton of certified e-waste processed through verifiable manifests (`Form 6`), authorized recyclers generate and sell EPR credits to brand owners at **₹3,000–₹6,000 per MT**.
- Part of this EPR compliance premium is passed back directly to the grassroots collector in the form of guaranteed minimum purchase prices.
